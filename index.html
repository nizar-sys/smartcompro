#FAQ

Frequently Ask Question

What is Smartin Technology System?

Smartin Technology System is a software development company that provides software solutions to businesses and individuals.

What services does Smartin Technology System offer?

We offer a range of services including software development, system integration, custom software solutions, and technical support for trading platforms such as MT4 and MT5.

What is the difference between MT4 and MT5?

MT4 is a trading platform focused on forex trading with a simpler structure, while MT5 offers multi-asset trading (stocks, commodities, forex) with more advanced features, faster processing, and enhanced trading tools.

Is support available 24/7?

Yes, Smartin Technology System provides 24/7 support for critical issues, ensuring that clients receive timely assistance whenever they need it..
